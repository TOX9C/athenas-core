The Mark

Pure black ink, engraved. Never recolored, tinted, gradiented, stroked, or shadowed. Clear space = the height of the plume. Minimum 24px.

no recolor, no gradient, no effects

Voice

Calm, exact, unhurried. A strategist briefing a peer.

Athena says

  • One window. Every tool. No noise.
  • ~15 MB. Native. No telemetry.
  • Built in Rust. Finished on purpose.
  • macOS Apple Silicon, for now — Linux and Windows are in progress.

Never

  • Blazingly fast! Revolutionary! Game-changer! 🚀
  • Crush your workflow and destroy the competition
  • No exclamation marks · no emoji · no hype adjectives
  • No hedging — one honest caveat, stated once
