Every tool. One window.

Athena’s Core is a native macOS workspace for people who command armies of AI agents. Multi-pane terminals, workspace-aware chat, a kanban board, and swarm orchestration — fused into a single, fast, beautiful window. No browser. No Electron. No mercy on your RAM.

III · THE PANTHEON

Four gods in one window

Not a bundle of apps glued with wishful thinking — one native surface where each discipline keeps its craft.

▲

Terminals

The Forge

Multi-pane native terminals with splits, search, and per-pane agent detection. When an agent works, the pane glows — gold while thinking, amber when it needs you.

Cmd+T to summon
◉

Athena Chat

The Oracle

Workspace-aware AI chat that reads your project and writes with context. Local models or remote APIs — she speaks to whatever you trust.

Cmd+K to consult
▦

Kanban

The Agora

Tasks argued in the open. Drag work between columns while terminals and agents watch the same board — no sync rituals, no export ceremonies.

Cmd+B to convene
✦

Swarm

The Phalanx

Orchestrate many agents across panes and spaces. Status on the dock badge, motion in the menu bar, verdicts in your face — never in your way.

Cmd+Shift+S to march
